## 描述 / Description

### 中文

塑灵模玩《白昼流星》1/12 拼装模型里连接翅膀与背包的径向轴。官方不提供 STL，也没有单独补件，件丢了或断了只能自己解决 —— 这是按照实物照片逆向、并用实际打印验证过的替代件。

**结构**（单位 mm，全部按照片定标）
- 两层，总厚 4.0：底层齿轮盘 2.0 + 上层圆环 2.0
- 底层齿轮盘 Ø12（含齿）；环带 1.5 = 光滑台肩 0.6 + 齿高 0.9，齿根 Ø10.2
- 齿：8 个，只在 160° 扇区内，齿距 20°，扇区中心与轴相反，其余为光滑圆边
- 上层圆环 Ø9，中心通孔 Ø5，孔口倒角 0.4
- 径向轴 Ø5 × 长 11，与中心孔垂直，位于厚度中面

**打印验证结果**
- Ø5 整圆轴 + Ø5 孔：装得上，不松动 → 本模型就是这一版
- 轴上下切平版：实测**会松动**，已放弃

**打印建议**
- 齿轮面朝下平放、轴水平；轴比本体上下各凸出 0.5 mm，圆盘底面会离床 0.5 mm，开支撑
- PETG 或 PA-CF（PLA 轴根偏脆）；层高 0.12–0.16，壁厚 ≥ 3 圈
- 太紧/太松只调孔径或轴径（±0.05 mm），不要切轴

**注意**：非官方配件，与塑灵模玩无关；仅供个人修复使用，禁止商用转售。CC BY-NC-SA 4.0。参数化源文件与照片对照：https://github.com/102637111/bzliuxing-wing-backpack-shaft

### English

Replacement for the radial shaft that connects the wings to the backpack on the SOULMOULD 白昼流星 1/12 plastic model kit. No official STL or spare part is sold, so this part was reverse-engineered from photos of the original and verified with a real print.

**Geometry** (mm)
- Two layers, 4.0 total: 2.0 gear disc + 2.0 upper ring
- Gear disc Ø12 including teeth; annular band 1.5 = 0.6 smooth shoulder + 0.9 tooth depth (root Ø10.2)
- 8 teeth inside a 160° sector, 20° pitch, opposite the shaft; smooth rim elsewhere
- Upper ring Ø9, Ø5 through bore, 0.4 chamfer
- Radial shaft Ø5 × 11, perpendicular to the bore, centered in the 4 mm stack

**Print-test result**
- Round Ø5 shaft + Ø5 bore: fits with no play — this is the version provided
- Flattened shaft variant: tested and **loose**, not recommended

**Printing**
- Gear face down, shaft horizontal; the shaft is 0.5 mm proud of both faces, so support the disc
- PETG or PA-CF (PLA is brittle at the shaft root); 0.12–0.16 mm layers, ≥ 3 walls
- Tune fit with bore/shaft diameter in 0.05 mm steps — don't cut the shaft

**Note**: unofficial fan part, not affiliated with SOULMOULD. Personal repair use only, no commercial resale. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Parametric source and photo comparison: https://github.com/102637111/bzliuxing-wing-backpack-shaft
